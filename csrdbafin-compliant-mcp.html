🚀 MCP Tools Inventory Standardized Interface

calculate_financed_emissions

Calculates PCAF Scope 3 financed emissions, intensity, and weighted data quality scores across Private Equity and Real Estate holdings.

Input: portfolioId, reportingYear, assetClassFilter?
Output: Cat 15 / Cat 13 emissions, GHG intensity, weighted DQS

audit_sfdr_classification

Audits SFDR 2.0 classification, flags compliance breaches, and calculates remediation CapEx required to maintain designated status.

Input: portfolioId, targetCategory (Article_6/8/9)
Output: Alignment percentage, detected breaches, CapEx summary

assess_crrem_stranding

Assesses stranding year, cumulative excess emissions, and carbon penalty liabilities against CRREM 1.5°C or 2.0°C decarbonization curves.

Input: portfolioId/assetId, pathwayType, scenario
Output: Stranding year, excess GHG tons, carbon liability (€)

calculate_brown_discount

Quantifies brown discount and derives stranding-adjusted Net Asset Value (NAV) based on retrofit CapEx, yield expansion, and carbon drag.

Input: portfolioId, targetYear, discountRate, yieldExpansion
Output: CapEx drag, yield impact, adjusted NAV

extract_epc_rating

Extracts certified EPC ratings, energy demands (primary/final), and CO2 performance from the German DIBt registry or uploaded certificates.

Input: assetId?, dibRegistrationNumber?
Output: EPC rating, energy demand (kWh/m²a), verified status

generate_csrd_statement

Generates a formal CSRD ESRS E1 Climate sustainability disclosure structure with auto-referenced portfolio calculations.

Input: portfolioId, reportingYear, includeOptions
Output: ESRS E1 outline with embedded audit payloads
